# SellerChamp Tools Suite v1.47

Freight Shipping Audit v1.1 adds an adjustable minimum weight in pounds (default 80). The audit includes products strictly above that weight, only after their live SellerChamp quantity_available is verified greater than zero. Zero stock and unavailable stock are skipped and counted. Stock is displayed on results. The selected threshold and results are saved on the server. Run a new scan after installing to apply the new stock filter to previous results. eBay remains read-only.
